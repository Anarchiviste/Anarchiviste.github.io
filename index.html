Research

Currently pursuing a Master's degree in archival studies with a focus on digital technologies applied to history at the École nationale des chartes, I work at the intersection of the humanities and computer science. I am currently involved in projects combining technological innovation and cultural transmission, focusing on peer-to-peer culture in the early 2000s and 2010s.

I won the grant for publication from the Association of French Archivists, which will lead to the publication of a scholarly article in La Gazette des archives.
