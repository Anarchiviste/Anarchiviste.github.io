Research & Activities

Intership at LIGM-Imagine : much fun so far

Computer vision isn't part of my core expertise. While I have some basic knowledge, I'm much more comfortable with text data processing. So far, things are going great; I'm producing a historical dataset to train detection models. I should be getting into the nitty-gritty of detection in a few weeks.

Piracy is part of our heritage, and I'm tired of hearing otherwise.

I am currently writing a thesis and an article on the role of piracy in the evolution of digital heritage. To summarize, I do not believe that piracy is a "necessary evil" but rather an integral part of the process of the cultural heritage status of intangible objects (films, series, books).
